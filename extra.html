Greater East Texas Information Technology

getIT is a committee that organizes community events to raise money for technology scholarships for local students.

Event Marketing
Brand Communications
Thought Leadership

As a marketing consultant for getIT, I developed promotional materials for two of the organization’s flagship events: the getIT on the Green golf outing and the Tyler Tech Expo. Both events were designed to educate local businesses on emerging technologies. I created all print collateral for these events—including flyers, banners, postcards, and event programs—ensuring a cohesive and professional brand presence across touchpoints.

Raving Fan Technologies

Raving Fan Technologies is an Information Technology company in Tyler, Texas.

Brand Strategy
Website Strategy

As a marketing consultant for Raving Fan Technologies, I led a full brand consolidation effort following the company’s acquisition of three legacy businesses. The challenge was to merge four outdated brands into a single, unified identity with a modern look and feel. I redesigned the website with updated copy and visuals—replacing content that, in some cases, hadn’t been refreshed since the late 1990s.



Central Florida Zoo & Botanical Gardens

The Central Florida Zoo & Botanical Gardens is a nonprofit, AZA-accredited organization.

UX Strategy
User-Centered Design
Information Architecture

For the Central Florida Zoo & Botanical Gardens website redesign, I led a data-informed website redesign project that guided every phase of research, discovery, and execution. Beginning with an in-depth analysis of user behavior, site performance, and stakeholder goals, I identified opportunities to improve navigation, accessibility, and engagement across a vast ecosystem of content. I created wireframes to simplify the visitor experience, then designed a fresh, modern interface. The result is a dynamic, user-friendly website that elevates the zoo’s digital presence while staying true to its playful, nature-inspired identity.

Time Square Vapor Lounge

Times Square Vapor Lounge is an e-cigarette retailer in Texas.

Marketing
Web & Print Design

Times Square Vapor Lounge needed a distinctive identity to stand out as new vapor lounges entered the market. Using the client’s red-and-black palette, I designed a versatile logo featuring a bold “V” integrated into the “M” to create an iconic, recognizable mark. I also produced cohesive digital and print collateral, including ads, social media graphics, and posters, to establish strong brand association and support the lounge’s ongoing marketing needs.
